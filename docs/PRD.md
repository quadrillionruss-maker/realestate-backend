# PRD — Nigeria Developer Edition v1

## ICP
Nigerian property developers & estate companies selling off-plan/installment:
2–10 active projects, 30–500 installment buyers, currently on Excel/WhatsApp
or outgrowing RealtyTracka-class record-keeping tools.

## Problem
Installment sales over 12–36 months create: missed/late payments nobody
chases systematically, double-allocated units, allocation documents stuck
in limbo, zero daily operational visibility for the MD/CEO.

## v1 scope (ALL of this, NOTHING more)
Login → Companies → Projects → Units → Customers → Sales reps →
Reservations → Installments → Payments (Paystack + manual) → Documents →
Tasks → Dashboard → **one AI worker** (daily brief, risk flags, drafted
WhatsApp/email follow-ups, auto-created tasks).

## Explicit non-goals for v1
Specialist agents, agent orchestration/execution, MLS, US edition,
multi-country config, marketplace/plugins, voice, in-app WhatsApp sending
(v1 drafts + copy button; WhatsApp Business API is a v1.x decision).

## Success metrics (first 90 days after first customer)
- 1 developer running a real project's full buyer list in the system
- ≥80% of that project's payments recorded through the platform
- Overdue follow-up sent (from a draft) within 48h of due date ≥90% of the time
- Zero double allocations
- CEO opens the dashboard ≥4 days/week

## Pricing hypothesis (validate in discovery, don't ship yet)
Per-project or per-active-buyer monthly; anchor against RealtyTracka's
~₦350k/yr with the AI layer as the premium justification.
