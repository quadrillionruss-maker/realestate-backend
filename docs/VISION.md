# Vision

## One line
The AI Operating System for real estate businesses — starting as the
sales-operations brain for Nigerian property developers.

## The end state (5 years)
A developer/CEO opens the platform at 8:00 AM. The **Deal Manager** has
already: read overnight payments and messages, updated every reservation,
flagged at-risk buyers, chased overdue installments, prepared allocation
documents, drafted client responses, and produced an executive summary with
recommended actions. Humans approve; AI executes.

## The wedge (now — Nigeria Developer Edition)
Nigerian developers selling off-plan/installment property run their most
critical process — buyer payment plans across 12–36 months — on Excel and
WhatsApp. Existing tools (RealtyTracka, OurProperty NG, Edynamics) digitize
records. **None of them think.** Our differentiation is the AI layer:
proactive risk-flagging, drafted follow-ups, a daily operational brief —
on top of solid installment/allocation rails.

## Why we win
1. **Reused infrastructure** — payments (Paystack), PDF generation, auth,
   email are proven, reused infrastructure rather than new surface area.
2. **AI-native, not AI-bolted-on** — the daily brief job is the embryonic
   Deal Manager; every future agent reports into the same brain.
3. **Founder proximity** — we can sit in a Lagos/Abuja sales office and
   watch the workflow. US competitors can't.

## Positioning
AI Operating System, not a CRM. The product's job is to *run operations*
and ask humans for approval — not to be another database humans must drive.

## What success looks like for v1
A developer with 2 projects and 80 installment buyers replaces their
tracking spreadsheet, stops double-allocating units, collects overdue
payments faster because follow-ups are drafted every morning, and checks
ONE screen daily instead of five.
