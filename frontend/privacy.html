Privacy Policy

Last updated: [effective date — set on publish]

This Privacy Policy explains how Archta ("Archta", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and protects personal data belonging to (a) the staff of a real estate development or sales business who use Archta to run their operations ("Workspace Users"), and (b) the buyers, tenants and prospects whose records a Workspace User enters into Archta ("Buyers"). It applies to the Archta web application and buyer portal. It does not apply to any third-party site you reach by following a link out of Archta.

Archta is operated by [Archta operating entity — not yet incorporated], a company being incorporated in Nigeria. Until incorporation is complete, this policy is published on behalf of that future entity.

1. Who controls this data

For Buyer data, the Workspace User (the real estate developer or sales business) is the data controller under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation — they decide what buyer information is collected and why. Archta is the data processor: we hold and process that data on the Workspace User's instructions, as set out in our Terms & Conditions. If you are a Buyer and want to exercise a data-protection right, you may contact either the Workspace User directly or us at the address in Section 10 below, and we will route your request appropriately.

For Workspace User account data (the staff who sign in to Archta), Archta is the data controller.

2. What we collect

Workspace User accounts: full name, email address, phone number, password (stored as a salted scrypt hash, never in plain text), profile photo, company name/branding details, and records of which workspace(s) and role(s) you hold.

Buyer / tenant records, entered by a Workspace User: full name, phone number, email address, the unit(s) reserved or rented, installment or rent schedules, payment history and amounts, documents generated for the buyer (allocation letters, receipts, demand letters and similar), and communications sent to the buyer (email/SMS/WhatsApp) and their delivery status.

Payment data: Archta does not collect or store card numbers or bank credentials. Online payments are processed by Paystack, either the Workspace User's own Paystack account or, if they haven't configured one, Archta's own. Archta records the outcome of a payment (amount, reference, date, status), not the payment instrument used.

Usage and device data: we do not run analytics or advertising trackers. Application logs may record IP address, browser/device information and error details for the sole purpose of operating and securing the service — see Section 6.

3. Why we process this data

  • To provide the core service: recording reservations, installment/rent schedules, payments, and generating the documents a sale or tenancy requires.
  • To send transactional communications a Buyer would reasonably expect: payment receipts, balance and overdue reminders, allocation letters, and portal access links.
  • To authenticate Workspace Users and enforce who on a team can see what, based on their role.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations, including keeping financial records auditable.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use Buyer data for advertising.

4. Who else processes this data on our behalf

Archta uses the following subprocessors to run the service. Each only receives the data it needs to perform its function:

  • Supabase (database and file storage) — hosts all application data.
  • Paystack (payment processing) — processes online card/bank payments; receives buyer contact details and payment amounts needed to complete a transaction.
  • Resend (transactional email) — sends receipts, reminders and portal links.
  • Termii (SMS) — sends text-message reminders and alerts, where configured.
  • OpenAI (optional) — where a workspace's AI daily brief feature is active, summarizes operational data (payment status, overdue amounts) to draft internal follow-up notes. Not used to make automated decisions about a Buyer, and every draft it produces is reviewed by a human before it is sent.
  • Google — if "Sign in with Google" is enabled for a workspace, Google processes the sign-in on our behalf. Loading our fonts from Google Fonts also causes your browser to make a request to Google's servers, which necessarily includes your IP address; see Section 7.

We do not permit any subprocessor to use this data for its own purposes.

5. How long we keep it

Financial and transactional records (payments, receipts, allocation letters, the audit log) are retained indefinitely, even after a record is deleted from the application's own view. This is deliberate: a receipt or allocation letter must be able to survive to settle a dispute years later, and Nigerian tax and company-law recordkeeping obligations require it. Deleting a buyer record in Archta marks it hidden and restorable ("soft-deleted"); it is not immediately erased from the database or from backups.

There is currently no self-service "delete my account" or "forget me" tool. If you are a Buyer and want your personal data erased under your right to erasure (Section 8), contact us using the details in Section 10 and we will process the request manually, together with the Workspace User where their own recordkeeping obligations apply. We will tell you if a legal or tax obligation means some records must be retained despite your request, and which ones.

6. Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text. A workspace's own third-party API keys (for example, a Paystack secret key) are encrypted at rest and never shown again once saved. Access to Buyer data is restricted by role within a workspace — for example, a Documentation role never sees payment amounts. Every sign-in session can be invalidated instantly workspace-wide, for example when a password is changed or a team member is removed. Application error logs are written to record what went wrong, not to capture personal data, and our internal practice is to log human-readable error messages rather than raw database errors that could otherwise quote a record's actual contents.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to us.

7. Cookies and local storage

Archta does not use cookies, and does not use any advertising or analytics tracker. Signing in stores a session token and your selected workspace in your browser's local storage, purely so you stay signed in between visits — this is strictly functional and is not shared with anyone. It stays on your device and is cleared when you sign out or clear your browser's site data.

Loading this page's typefaces from Google Fonts causes your browser to connect directly to Google's servers, which sees your IP address as part of that request, independently of Archta. If "Sign in with Google" is enabled, choosing it loads a script from Google and shares your Google account information with us to create or verify your sign-in, governed by Google's own privacy policy.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you may have the right to: access the personal data we (or, for Buyer data, the relevant Workspace User) hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; object to or restrict certain processing; request erasure, subject to Section 5 above; and request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these, contact us using Section 10. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

9. Children

Archta is a business tool and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect data from, children.
