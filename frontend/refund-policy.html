Refund Policy

Last updated: [effective date — set on publish]

This policy covers subscription fees you pay Archta for use of the Service. It does not cover money a Buyer pays you (a developer or sales business) for a unit, installment, or rent through Archta — those are payments in a sale or tenancy between you and your own buyer, and any refund of them is between you and that buyer, governed by your own sale or tenancy agreement, not by Archta.

1. No refunds on subscription fees

Subscription fees are billed in advance for the billing period you choose and are non-refundable, including for a partially used period. You may cancel your subscription at any time; cancellation stops future billing but does not refund fees already paid for the current period, and you keep access through the end of that period.

2. Exceptions

The one exception is a proven billing error — for example, being charged twice for the same period, or charged after a cancellation that took effect before the billing date. Report this to us using the contact below within [dispute window — placeholder, e.g. 30 days] of the charge, and if we confirm the error, we will refund the erroneous charge.

3. Failed or disputed card charges

If a card payment for your subscription is reversed by your bank or card network (a chargeback) without first contacting us, we may suspend your workspace until the matter is resolved.

4. How to request a refund review

Contact [billing contact email — placeholder] with your workspace name, the charge date, and the reason you believe it was made in error. We will respond within a reasonable time.
