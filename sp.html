Executive Summary

One system for the whole sale — not five spreadsheets and a WhatsApp group.

Most Nigerian developers running installment sales over 12–36 months are doing it across a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp thread, and whoever on the team remembers to follow up. It works — until a unit gets reserved twice, an allocation letter goes missing, or a director asks "how much came in this week" and nobody has a clean answer without opening three files.

Archta replaces that entire stack with one connected system, and adds something no spreadsheet and no generic property CRM can: a layer that reviews every buyer's payment behaviour every single morning, flags who is quietly falling behind before it becomes a write-off, and hands your team a drafted follow-up instead of a blank message box. This proposal walks through what that looks like for Fhemfel Homes — project by project, buyer by buyer.

The Problem

What running installment sales on spreadsheets and WhatsApp actually costs.

If any of the following sounds familiar, it's not a Fhemfel Homes problem — it's the default state of off-plan sales at nearly every Nigerian developer that hasn't yet moved off manual tracking.

A payment plan running 12–36 months, tracked in a spreadsheet only one person on the team really understands.
Follow-ups that depend on someone remembering to chase — not a system that never forgets.
A unit reserved twice because two staff were working from the same file at the same time.
Allocation letters, receipts and contracts scattered across email threads and phone photos.
No single screen a Managing Director can open at 8am and actually trust.
Handover, snagging, legal recovery and contractor payments each living in a different notebook — if they live anywhere at all.

The Solution

Archta, from first inquiry to final handover — and beyond.

Archta covers the entire lifecycle of an off-plan, outright or rental sale as one connected system, not a patchwork of apps. What follows is a walk through everything it does for your sales, collections, documentation and leadership teams.

01

Inventory, Reservations & Allocation

Every project, unit and reservation in one place — with the one rule that matters enforced at the deepest level of the system.

  • Projects and units with rich profiles — floor plans, photos, specifications, price and status — not a spreadsheet tab per project
  • A unit can never be reserved twice: a second attempt on an already-taken unit is rejected outright, even if two staff click "reserve" in the same second
  • Off-plan, outright-purchase and rental deals run through the same underlying system — no separate tool to learn per deal type
  • Every reservation carries its full history permanently — who sold it, when, on what plan
02

Buyers & the Sales Pipeline

Buyers and your sales team, tracked with the discipline of a CRM built specifically for this business.

  • A complete buyer record: contact details, every reservation they hold, every note ever logged against them
  • A free-text activity log for calls, visits and WhatsApp conversations — written down once, visible to the whole team
  • An automatic credit score for every buyer, built entirely from their own payment history — no external bureau involved
  • A referral network — every buyer gets a shareable code the moment they're added, so word-of-mouth sales are tracked
  • A blacklist that hard-stops a known bad actor from being reserved onto a new unit
03

Installment Plans, Payments & Collections

This is the core of the business, and it's built to the kobo.

  • Installment schedules calculated precisely in kobo, correctly handling month-end dates (a plan due the 31st still lands sensibly in February)
  • Payments in by card and bank transfer through Paystack, or recorded manually for cash, POS and direct transfer — every route lands on the same schedule
  • An overpayment is never lost or force-fit onto the wrong installment — it's held as real, recorded money until your team applies it deliberately
  • A wrongly entered payment can be voided and corrected without ever deleting the original record — the true financial history stays intact
  • A payment plan can be renegotiated mid-way through — everything already paid stays exactly as it happened; a new plan covers what's left
  • A formal, owner-approved pause for a buyer who genuinely can't pay for a month or two, so real hardship doesn't quietly become a default
  • Every missed payment escalates on a ladder — gentle, then firmer, then a management flag — and every "I'll pay Friday" promise is tracked so it can't slip
04

Documents, Contracts & E-Signatures

The paperwork side of a sale, generated and signed by the same system that tracks the money.

  • Allocation letters and receipts generate automatically, and can only ever exist once per reservation and once per payment — no conflicting duplicates
  • Contracts of sale, deeds of assignment, subscriber agreements and powers of attorney, generated from your own data
  • Buyers review and countersign electronically — no printing, scanning or physical meeting required
  • A document left unsigned too long is flagged automatically rather than quietly going stale
  • Every version of every document is kept, and it carries your own letterhead and wording, not a generic template
05

Construction, Handover & Contractors

Archta follows a unit past the sale, through the build, to the day a buyer moves in.

  • Five-stage construction tracking per project — Foundation, Superstructure, Roofing, Finishing, Handover — with target dates, actual dates and progress photos
  • A daily project-health score, so a project quietly slipping behind schedule shows up as a trend before it becomes a crisis on launch day
  • A handover checklist and snagging list per unit, so defects reported at move-in are tracked to resolution, not lost in a WhatsApp thread
  • Contractor and supplier payments tracked against the same milestones — the outflow side of the ledger, next to the inflow side
06

Legal & Recovery

When a buyer relationship reaches formal recovery, that process has a home too.

  • A legal case tracker, opened deliberately once a file is actually with a lawyer — never automatically
  • Lawyer contact details, case status and every update recorded against the buyer's own record, permanently
07

Bank Financing Support

Not every buyer pays outright — Archta helps the ones financing their purchase.

  • A buyer applies for financing from their own portal, using their genuine Archta payment history as proof of creditworthiness
  • Your team reviews and takes the application to the buyer's bank, with the request tracked from submission to decision
08

Rental & Tenancy Management

For units you let rather than sell, the same installment engine simply runs on rent.

  • A tenancy's monthly rent schedule tracked exactly like an installment plan — same overdue logic, same receipts
  • Renewal is its own clean process — a fresh lease term, with the old one's history left untouched
  • The morning brief flags a lease 60 days before it ends, prompting a renew-or-vacate decision before it's urgent
09

The AI Sales Operations Manager

This is what actually separates Archta from a system that only records what already happened.

  • Every morning, before your team opens their laptops, Archta has already read yesterday's payments, flagged every buyer falling behind, and drafted a follow-up message for each one
  • A sales forecast — pipeline and expected collections — generated and saved, so this month's projection can be honestly compared against what actually happened
  • Smart plan suggestions when a rep is structuring a new reservation, informed by what similar buyers have actually been able to sustain
  • Every AI output is a proposal, never an autopilot — nothing reaches a buyer until someone on your team approves it
  • The daily brief is the foundation of a wider AI workforce already taking shape underneath it — specialist collections, documentation and scheduling agents reporting into the same brain, so Archta only gets more capable from here, never more complicated to use
10

The Buyer Portal

A clean, self-service view for your buyers — fewer "please resend my receipt" calls for your team.

  • A live dashboard of their own payment schedule, history and outstanding balance
  • Every document generated for them, available to download on demand
  • A direct message thread to your team, a project community space, and a notification bell
  • Their own place to submit a financing or hardship request, and to tell you how their handover went
11

Team, Roles & Multi-Branch Structure

An access model that matches how a Nigerian developer's office is actually staffed.

  • Five roles — Owner, Sales Director, Sales Rep, Collections, Documentation — each seeing exactly what their job requires
  • A documentation officer never sees a naira figure; a sales rep sees only their own buyers, never a colleague's book
  • A company with more than one office runs as one group account holding independent branch workspaces, each with its own team and numbers
  • New team members join through a signed, time-limited invite link, with clear rules on who may invite whom into which role
12

Reporting & Business Intelligence

The numbers a developer actually needs to run the business, ready without anyone building a spreadsheet.

  • Investor and partner-ready reports, and cash-flow forecasts built directly from your live installment schedules
  • Collections performance, a payment heatmap of when money actually comes in, and a sales-rep leaderboard
  • Referral performance and post-handover buyer satisfaction, tracked as real numbers rather than anecdote
  • A custom export to CSV whenever a number is needed somewhere else
13

Communication, On Every Channel

Reaching a buyer the way Nigerian buyers actually expect to be reached.

  • WhatsApp messages that can be scheduled ahead, not only sent in the moment
  • Email and SMS on your own accounts if you have them, or ours if you don't — nothing to set up before day one
  • Your own wording on outgoing emails and receipts, not a fixed template you can't touch
  • Browser push notifications for anything that needs a team member's attention immediately
14

Security, Trust & Data Integrity

This system holds other people's money and their legal paperwork. It's built like it knows that.

  • Two-factor authentication, and a visible list of every active session with the ability to sign any of them out remotely
  • The moment a password changes or someone leaves the team, every token they were ever issued stops working immediately — not in thirty days
  • A workspace's own payment and messaging credentials, if you choose to use your own accounts, are encrypted and never shown in plain text again
  • Nothing is ever truly deleted — every record and every action stays in the system permanently and can be restored
  • The database itself, not just the application on top of it, refuses a double allocation, a duplicate receipt, or a sales rep being paid commission twice on the same transfer
